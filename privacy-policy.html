VKT LinkClean Privacy Policy

VKT LinkClean does NOT collect, store, or transmit your links, your browsing data or any personal information. Every rule runs inside your browser: no redirect is fetched, no link is uploaded, and cleaning, rules and history work offline. The only network request the extension makes is the optional Pro licence check described in Section 4.

01Data Collection

VKT LinkClean does not collect personal information, browsing history, page content or usage analytics. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting and no telemetry of any kind. The links you clean are processed in the popup and, if you keep a history, stored only in this browser's local extension storage.

02How VKT LinkClean Works

Everything happens inside your browser:

  • Input — you paste a URL, press Paste to read the clipboard, or pull the URL of the tab you are on.
  • Cleaning — the URL is parsed with the browser's own URL API, tracking parameters are matched against 61 built-in names and 12 prefix families plus any rules you added, and the result is rebuilt.
  • Redirects — a wrapper link (l.facebook.com, google.com/url, out.reddit.com and 15 other shapes) is unpacked by reading the target out of the URL itself. The redirect is never requested, so the destination site does not learn that you looked at it.
  • Output — the clean URL, a Markdown link, a text or CSV export, or an entry in your local history.
No link, URL or page content is ever sent to a server, including ours.

04Third-Party Services & Licence Verification

Free features make no network request at all. With a Pro licence, the extension contacts our licence server (api.annmax1983.com) only to activate the key, to re-verify it at most once every two hours when you open the popup, and to release it. Those requests carry the licence key, a random installation identifier, the plugin id and basic browser metadata (browser name and version, interface language, time zone, user agent) — never a link, a page address or your history. Our licence server records the IP address of these requests solely for rate limiting and abuse prevention, never for profiling, advertising or sharing.

If the check fails, nothing is locked: the cached licence keeps working offline.

05Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

06Data Retention & Deletion

History, settings, custom rules and counters live in this browser's local extension storage. Clear the history from the popup, or remove the extension, and the data is gone with it — there is no copy anywhere else. Export a rules bundle or the history JSON first if you want to keep something.

07Children's Privacy

VKT LinkClean does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
